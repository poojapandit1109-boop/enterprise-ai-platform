Company Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval.

Remote work requests should be submitted at least two business days in advance.

Employees working remotely are expected to remain available during normal
working hours from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Employees must have a reliable internet connection and a suitable workspace
when working remotely.

Managers may require employees to work from the office when business needs
require in-person collaboration.

Remote work privileges may be reviewed periodically based on performance,
business requirements, and team needs.
